What we build

Most apps rent your attention and keep your life on their servers. Capricorn builds the opposite: Caps you launch from Capricorn OS or install as a PWA, with plain language about what stays on your device — and what, if anything, leaves it.

From an encrypted vault to training logs, recovery tools, PSX ledgers, travel folders, and family cookbooks — each Cap looks different on purpose. The only shared rule is Device Sovereignty.
