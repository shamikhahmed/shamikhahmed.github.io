IdeaCap Privacy

Effective 15 September 2026 · Published by Capricorn Systems, Karachi, Pakistan

Summary

IdeaCap works on your device. We don't have accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and we don't sell data.

What stays on your device

  • Idea notes (title, transcript, tags, summary, chat)
  • Optional audio recordings you capture
  • Folders and soft reminders
  • Optional JSON backups you create yourself

What leaves your device

ServiceWhat is sentWhyWhen
Nothing——IdeaCap makes no network calls for your ideas. Static app files load from GitHub Pages. Speech recognition, when available, runs on device.

Microphone & speech

Microphone access is used only to record voice notes you start. On-device speech recognition (when the OS provides it) stays on the device. On the web, type your idea — browser cloud speech is not used.

Your controls

Export your data: Settings → Data → Export backup. Erase all data: Settings → Data → Erase all data.

Children

IdeaCap is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over.

Changes

We'll update this page and its effective date if anything changes.

Contact

Support: shamikhahmed.github.io/support.html (GitHub issues on the Capricorn hub). No personal email or phone is published.
