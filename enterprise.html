30-minute investor walkthrough

Repeatable demo across all eight Cap apps. Each product has its own personality — explore the product pages first, then launch live apps with seed data below.

Before you start (5 min)

  1. Chrome or Safari on Mac/iPhone; network for first PWA load only.
  2. Optional local serve: npx serve -l 8765 . per app folder.
  3. Playwright: npx playwright install chromium in any app repo.

FAQ

Is data encrypted?

VaultCap: AES-256-GCM. Others: localStorage/IndexedDB — protect with device passcode.

Telemetry?

No analytics SDKs in any app. Zero data leaves the device.

Medical device?

No. SteadyCap SOS is not clinical care.

Investment advice?

No. LedgerCap is a personal ledger.

AI?

VaultCap optional LLM import. PulseCap Smart Coach is rule-based.

Offline?

All eight PWAs work offline after first load — Device Sovereignty is the one shared rule.

Verification
