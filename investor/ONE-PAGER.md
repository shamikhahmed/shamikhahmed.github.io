## What

Capricorn Systems builds **Cap** apps — premium personal software that lives on the user’s device. Offline-first PWAs. No mandatory cloud account. One company rule: **Device Sovereignty**.

Founder: **Shamikh Ahmed**. Capricorn Systems is separate from NEWS Logistics / TheSolution360 / MTX.

---

## Why it matters

Most consumer apps rent user data and break without servers. Caps are install-once (Add to Home Screen), keep working offline, and keep secrets on-device. Product family shares Capricorn craft without looking like a white-label clone.
