FAQ

Is data encrypted? VaultCap: AES-256-GCM. Others: localStorage/IndexedDB — protect device passcode.
Telemetry? No analytics SDKs in any app.
Medical device? No. SteadyCap SOS is not clinical care.
Investment advice? No. LedgerCap is a personal ledger.
AI? VaultCap optional LLM import. PulseCap Smart Coach is rule-based.
Offline? All six PWAs work offline after first load — the one shared rule: Device Sovereignty.
