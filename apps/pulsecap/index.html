    Put it on your Home Screen

    PulseCap is a web app — installing takes a few seconds and it opens full-screen, offline.

    1. iPhone & iPad

      Open PulseCap in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

    2. Mac

      In Safari choose File → Add to Dock. In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon in the address bar.

    3. Android

      In Chrome, open the ⋮ menu and tap Install app.

    Questions

    Is it free?

    Yes. Every feature, no ads, no subscriptions, no account.

    Where is my data?

    Workouts, plans, PRs, body measurements, progress photos and nutrition. It lives in this browser on this device — clearing site data erases it, so export a backup first.

    Is Smart Coach an AI?

    No. Smart Coach is rules applied to your own training history, on your phone.

    Do I need a wearable?

    No. Recovery readiness comes from your logs and check-ins.

    Is this medical advice?

    PulseCap gives general training guidance, not medical advice. Stop and get medical help if you feel pain, dizziness or chest discomfort.

    Does it work offline?

    Yes, after the first visit.
