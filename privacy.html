Privacy

Capricorn Systems builds offline-first Cap apps. This page is the company truth — not a cookie banner cosplay.

Last updated: 19 July 2026

What we collect on Cap PWAs

Nothing by default. VaultCap, PulseCap, PrismCap, SteadyCap, LedgerCap, DeePonyCap, ScentCap, AuraCap, MasteryCap, SoulCap (and siblings) keep data on your device (localStorage / IndexedDB). No Capricorn account wall. No Capricorn analytics SDK in those PWAs.

This marketing site

The Capricorn hub (this domain) is a static GitHub Pages site. It does not require login. Browser may send normal web logs to GitHub Pages infrastructure; Capricorn Systems does not run a first-party tracker on these pages.

Demos

Try Demo links open sample data locally in your browser. Most Caps use ?demo=1. Where a PIN is required (VaultCap), the demo PIN is 123456 — Vault-only, not used by other Caps. Demo data is not your real life — and is not uploaded to Capricorn servers.

Optional third parties

Some Caps may optionally call services you choose (for example market pulse workers, or LLM APIs with your keys). Those calls leave your device only when you turn them on.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on the relevant Cap GitHub repo, or reach Shamikh Ahmed via Connect links on the hub.
