## Quick tips

1. **Add to Home Screen** (Safari Share → Add to Home Screen) so the Cap behaves like an app and works offline after the first load.
2. **VaultCap PIN:** after `?demo=1`, unlock with PIN **`123456`** (demo only).
3. **MasteryCap demo:** open https://shamikhahmed.github.io/MasteryCap/ → **Settings → Enter demo mode** (no query param).
4. Caps store data on your device. Clear site data / reset in-app if you want a clean slate.
