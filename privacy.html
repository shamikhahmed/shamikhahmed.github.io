Privacy

Effective 15 September 2026 · Published by Capricorn Systems, Karachi, Pakistan

Summary

This marketing hub and Capricorn OS shell are static pages on GitHub Pages. Cap PWAs work on your device. We don’t have accounts, analytics, ads or tracking on those PWAs, and we don’t sell data.

What stays on your device

Each Cap stores its own data locally (for example notes, workouts, holdings, journals). See that Cap’s privacy page from Settings → About for the exact list.

No Capricorn login. No first-party tracker. Browser may send normal web logs to GitHub Pages infrastructure.
