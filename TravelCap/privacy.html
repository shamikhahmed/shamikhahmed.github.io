TravelCap privacy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

TravelCap is an offline-first travel organizer. Trip data, packing lists, memories and documents are stored on your device (IndexedDB). There is no TravelCap account.

Optional network services

  • Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) — place search / geocoding when you look up locations.
  • REST Countries — country reference data.
  • Open-Meteo — weather forecasts for trip cities.
  • Frankfurter — currency conversion rates.
  • AviationStack — optional flight lookup. You may paste your own API key in Settings. The key stays on your device for static hosting; many AviationStack plans block browser CORS or HTTPS from the free tier — TravelCap shows a plain error and offers manual entry.

Those providers receive normal request metadata (IP, user agent) when a feature calls them.

Documents and OCR

Document photos stay on your device. Optional scan uses on-device OCR (Tesseract) loaded only when you tap Scan.

App lock

Optional passcode lock can encrypt travel documents on this device. If you forget the passcode, protected documents cannot be recovered — export a backup first.

Disclaimer

TravelCap helps you organize trips and documents. It is not a substitute for official travel advice, visas, or airline information. Verify times and requirements with carriers and authorities.
