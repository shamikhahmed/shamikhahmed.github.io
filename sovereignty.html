The one rule

Device Sovereignty

Your Cap data stays on your device. No mandatory account. Works offline after first load. Everything else about each Cap is intentionally different.

Enter Capricorn OS Browse Caps
On your deviceData is stored locally — encrypted where it matters. We don’t run a sync cloud for your vault, workouts, games, or ledgers.
Offline capableAfter first load, Caps keep working without connectivity. Airplane mode is a feature.
No mandatory accountInstall and use. No email wall, no OAuth gate, no “sign in to continue.”
You own the filesExport, backup, delete — your device, your rules. We don’t hold your data hostage.

Device Sovereignty isn’t anti-cloud dogma — it’s a design default. When your finances, recovery journal, or collection lives on hardware you control, the threat model changes. Capricorn OS’s Privacy Monitor counts third-party requests on this visit so privacy claims stay measured, not invented.

Some Caps optionally talk to first-party workers (prices, flights, Smart Import) when you choose — each Cap’s privacy section says what leaves the device.
