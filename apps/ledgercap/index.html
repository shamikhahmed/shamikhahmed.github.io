    Put it on your Home Screen

    LedgerCap is a web app — installing takes a few seconds and it opens full-screen, offline.

    1. iPhone & iPad

      Open LedgerCap in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

    2. Mac

      In Safari choose File → Add to Dock. In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon in the address bar.

    3. Android

      In Chrome, open the ⋮ menu and tap Install app.

    Questions

    Is it free?

    Yes. Every feature, no ads, no subscriptions, no account.

    Where is my data?

    Holdings, transactions, watchlists, notes and settings. It lives in this browser on this device — clearing site data erases it, so export a backup first.

    Are prices live?

    Prices are indicative and may be delayed. LedgerCap is for tracking and education. Prices may be delayed or indicative. Nothing here is investment advice.

    Does it support Urdu?

    Yes — English, اردو and Roman Urdu, in Settings → General.

    Does it work offline?

    Your portfolio does. Market prices need a connection.
