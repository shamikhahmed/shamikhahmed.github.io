# CarCap Privacy

Effective 15 September 2026 · Published by Capricorn Systems, Karachi, Pakistan

Hosted page: [privacy.html](./privacy.html)

## Summary
CarCap works on your device. We don't have accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and we don't sell data.

## What stays on your device
- Vehicles, service, fuel and document notes (`localStorage` key `carcap_v1`)
- Optional document photos (IndexedDB `carcap_photos`, max 2 MB each)
- Theme and reminder notification preference

## What leaves your device
Nothing. Static app files load from GitHub Pages only.

## Your controls
Export: Settings → Data → Export JSON. Erase: Settings → Data → Erase all data.
