Manifesto

Device Sovereignty

The one rule every Capricorn product shares. Everything else is intentionally different.

On your deviceData is stored locally — encrypted where it matters. We don't operate sync servers for your vault, workouts, games, or ledgers.
Offline capableAfter first load, Capricorn apps work without connectivity. Airplane mode is a feature, not a bug.
No mandatory accountInstall and use. No email wall, no OAuth gate, no "sign in to continue."
You own the filesExport, backup, delete — your device, your rules. We don't hold your data hostage.

Device Sovereignty isn't anti-cloud dogma — it's a design choice. When your finances, recovery journal, or child's collection lives on hardware you control, the threat model changes. Capricorn Systems builds for that model by default.
