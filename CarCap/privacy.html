CarCap Privacy

Effective 15 September 2026 · Published by Capricorn Systems, Karachi, Pakistan

Summary

CarCap works on your device. We don't have accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and we don't sell data.

What stays on your device

  • Vehicles (make, model, year, plate, nickname)
  • Service visits, reminder dates, fuel entries and document notes
  • Optional document photos stored in IndexedDB on this device
  • Theme and reminder notification preference
  • Optional JSON backups you create yourself

What leaves your device

ServiceWhat is sentWhyWhen
Nothing——CarCap makes no network calls for your data. Static app files load from GitHub Pages.

Your controls

Export your data: Settings → Data → Export JSON. Erase all data: Settings → Data → Erase all data. Optional notifications: Settings → Reminders.

Children

CarCap is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over.

Changes

We'll update this page and its effective date if anything changes.
